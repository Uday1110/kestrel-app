## Brand & Style

The design system projects high-velocity digital engineering, enterprise precision, and forward-looking technological intelligence. Tailored for enterprise decision-makers, engineering leaders, and transformation architects, the interface evokes sharp authority, dynamic energy, and clean operational reliability.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern High-Contrast Tech** with **Minimalist Architectural Clarity**. It anchors on a deep, obsidian-cast canvas illuminated by vibrant, energetic electrical accents. Rather than soft decorative layers, the system relies on deliberate structural discipline, stark geometric clarity, generous negative space, and vibrant focal points that draw immediate gaze toward critical calls-to-action and key metrics.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for visual impact on high-density technical interfaces:

- **Primary (`#02F576`)**: Kinetic Emerald. Reserved for focal interaction points, active states, progress indicators, and critical conversion triggers. It stands with sharp luminescence against dark backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#5F2CFF`)**: Hyper Violet. Used for secondary actions, deep tech highlights, badges, and gradient accents pairing against deep tones.
- **Neutral (`#0B0E14`)**: Void Black. The structural canvas base, establishing high dynamic range and immersive technological depth.
- **Slate Muted (`#B0B8C4`)**: Functional gray engineered for balanced secondary body copy, passive icons, and structural borders without visual glare.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary typography and high-contrast graphical elements.

Color application follows strict operational hierarchy: avoid overwhelming interfaces with full-bleed neon green; apply the primary color surgically to guide user flow through complex digital architectures.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Montserrat** across all roles to project an unapologetic, geometric, architectural cadence.

- **Headings & Display**: Bold, tightly kerned display scales provide weight and presence suited for thought-leadership statements and core transformation narratives.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: Regular weights maintain optical openness on deep-toned canvases. Generous line-heights prevent eye fatigue across dense technical whitepapers or solution architectures.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Uppercase styling with slight positive letter spacing is reserved for categorization tags, technical metrics, and navigational metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure employs a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for complex enterprise dashboards, solution matrices, and multi-tier tech case studies.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 48px canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Maximum content container width capped at 1360px for readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 32px canvas margin, 20px gutters. Content cards reflow into 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 20px canvas margin, 16px gutters. Structural groups collapse to single-column vertical flows.

Rhythmic vertical cadence follows strict 8px increments, utilizing `space-xl` for sectional transitions, `space-md` for component grouping, and `space-sm` for inline element pairings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on dark interfaces requires tonal stratification rather than heavy, muddy drop-shadows.

1. **Surface Layering**:
   - **Base (Canvas)**: `#0B0E14` (Deep Void).
   - **Level 1 (Cards, Modules)**: `#121722` (Subtle Dark Slate).
   - **Level 2 (Modals, Flyouts, Popovers)**: `#1A202E` (Elevated Deep Navy).

2. **Borders & Outlines**:
   Surfaces rely on razor-thin, semi-transparent ghost borders (`rgba(176, 184, 196, 0.12)`) to delineate edges against dark backgrounds without heavy visual clutter.

3. **Glow & Light Accents**:
   Interactive states and active indicators selectively emit low-spread, ambient colored luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.18)`), creating a sharp electrical aesthetic.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft/Technical** shape philosophy (`roundedness: 1`):

- Standard components (buttons, text inputs, chips) carry a crisp 4px border radius (`0.25rem`), reflecting precision engineering and structure.
- Large containers, cards, and modal dialogs use `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`) to soften structural blocks without compromising the architectural geometry.
- Avatars, status pills, and numeric indicator tags are allowed full pill radius to contrast sharply against rectangular content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#02F576` background, `#0B0E14` bold text, 4px radius. High kinetic glow on hover (`0 0 16px rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary**: Outlined in `rgba(176, 184, 196, 0.3)` on transparent background with `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, background transitions to `rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.15)` with a `#5F2CFF` border.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background with `#B0B8C4` text; text transitions to `#02F576` on interaction.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#121722` background and a 1px solid `rgba(176, 184, 196, 0.12)` border.
- Interactive cards feature a top accent hairline border that shifts to `#02F576` or `#5F2CFF` on hover with a slight 2px vertical lift.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status/Category Chips**: Compact height (24px/28px), 4px radius, tinted dark backgrounds (`rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.12)`), text rendered in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Metric Pill**: Pill-rounded capsule with a solid glowing dot (`#02F576`) representing live uptime, pipeline status, or algorithmic signals.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background of `#0E121A`, border of `1px solid rgba(176, 184, 196, 0.2)`, text in `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder copy in `#B0B8C4`.
- Focus state activates an instantaneous `#02F576` 1px border stroke with a soft 4px outward ambient glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with a 1px `rgba(176, 184, 196, 0.3)` border on `#0B0E14`.
- Checked state fills with `#02F576`, displaying a pitch-black `#0B0E14` interior checkmark or radio dot.

### Lists & Navigation
- Menu items sit in `#B0B8C4` with subtle horizontal tracking.
- Active list items receive an active left indicator border (2px solid `#02F576`) accompanied by pure white text.
